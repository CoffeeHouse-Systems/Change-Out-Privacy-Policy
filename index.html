Change Out Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Three’s Company (Catering) Limited, trading as Coffee House, is the data controller for Change Out. Change Out is an internal iPad app that authorised warehouse staff use to prepare and dispatch store change orders.

Data we use

  • Network and request information. The service receives your public IP address when the app connects and checks it against approved networks to decide whether to allow access. Hosting and service logs may include technical information about requests for security, reliability and troubleshooting.
  • Change orders and activity. The app retrieves existing records including store details, the requester’s work email or store identifier, order dates, denomination amounts and status. When staff edit an order, prepare a label, mark an order as sent or delete it, the service records the action, its time and the identity used for the action. Some actions also retain the order details before and after the change.
  • Print data. When you print a label, the app passes the label document to Apple’s print system and the selected AirPrint printer. The label includes store and order details and a QR code linked to the order.

Why we use this data

We use this information to restrict access, process and track store change orders, keep an operational record and resolve service or dispatch issues. Our legitimate interests are operating the change-order process, securing the service and maintaining accurate records. We also process information where necessary to meet legal obligations.

Who can access the data

Authorised Coffee House staff can access change-order information for their work. Cloudflare hosts the service and processes data on our behalf. We require service providers handling personal data on our behalf to protect it in line with this policy and applicable law. We may disclose information where legally required. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

We keep order and activity records for as long as needed for operations, reconciliation, audit and applicable legal requirements. Technical logs are kept according to the service’s logging settings and operational needs. Deleting an order in Change Out removes it from the pending list and preserves an audit record of the deletion.

Contact Coffee House if you want to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, or to ask us to restrict or stop certain uses. We assess requests against the records we need to retain for legal or legitimate operational reasons. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it through the same contact route.

Contact

Use Coffee House’s contact page for privacy questions or requests. Please mention Change Out so your request reaches the right team. You can also find more information in the Coffee House privacy policy.
